What clear aligners can help with

Clear aligners can gently move your teeth into a straighter position. They suit a range of cases, from small adjustments to the front teeth through to fuller alignment. At your assessment the dentist will confirm whether clear aligners are right for you.

Crowding

Teeth that overlap or sit too close together.

Gaps and spacing

Spaces between the teeth you would like to close.

The front ‘social six’

Minor adjustments to the front teeth, often the quickest cases.

Full-mouth alignment

A fuller correction across the whole smile.

What to expect

Your clear aligner journey

After your assessment and digital scan, a series of custom clear aligners is made for you. You wear each set in turn, around 20 to 22 hours a day, and your teeth move gradually towards their planned position. You come in for reviews along the way.

Treatment usually takes around six to eighteen months, depending on how much movement is needed, and up to around two years for full-mouth correction. Your own timeline is confirmed at your appointment with the dentist.
